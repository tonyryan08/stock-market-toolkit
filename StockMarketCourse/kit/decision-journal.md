# The Decision Journal

**Takeaway kit · page 1 · Intro to the Stock Market**

The one rule of this course that is never waived: **no entry, no trade.** Every paper trade gets a journal entry *before* execution. The journal is how we grade decisions instead of outcomes — because six months later, without it, your memory will quietly rewrite history in your favour. Everyone's does.

---

## Before the decision

| Field | Your entry |
|---|---|
| Date & time | |
| The decision, in one sentence | |
| What rule or signal triggered this? *(gut feeling is an answer — write it down honestly)* | |
| My thesis: why this, why now, in two sentences max | |
| What would prove me wrong? *(be specific — a price, a fact, a date)* | |
| Size, and why that size *(per the course rules once you have them)* | |
| How calm am I right now? *(1 = fully calm … 5 = excited or anxious)* | |
| Review date *(when will I judge this?)* | |

## At the review date

| Field | Your entry |
|---|---|
| Date | |
| What actually happened | |
| Did I follow my own rules? *(yes / no — no partial credit)* | |
| Grade the **process**: what would I repeat? What would I change? | |
| Was the outcome mostly luck or mostly skill — and how do I know? | |

---

## A worked example (everyday version, from lesson 1)

**Before —** *Date:* Tue 8 Sep, 7:05pm. *Decision:* Take the back road home instead of the N25. *Trigger:* Radio said roadworks at the viaduct. *Thesis:* Roadworks at rush hour usually cost me 20+ minutes; the back road is slower but steady. *Proves me wrong:* If the back road takes over 45 minutes, the detour wasn't worth it. *Size:* n/a — but note I'm risking 10 known minutes to avoid 20 uncertain ones. *Calm:* 2. *Review:* On arrival.

**After —** Took 38 minutes; a neighbour on the N25 took 31 — the roadworks had lifted. *Followed my rules?* Yes. *Process grade:* Good decision with the information I had at 7:05; I'd repeat it. Checking a live traffic app first would improve the process. *Luck or skill?* The outcome (losing 7 minutes) was luck — the roadworks happened to lift. The decision stands.

Notice what the journal just did: it let you lose and still be right. That is the entire course, in one car journey.
