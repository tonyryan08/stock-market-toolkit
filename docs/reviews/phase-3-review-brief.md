## The project in three sentences

A community-school course ("Intro to the Stock Market", Cork) with a Cloudflare Workers + D1 toolkit built week by week in public; paper portfolios only, "Education, not advice" on every page, process graded over outcomes. Phases 1–2 (weeks 1–6) built the nightly price robot, the books, Benjamin Graham's two screens and an AI explainer that is checked by the toolkit; Phase 3 (weeks 7–9) implemented Robert Carver's Starter System on US shares with no leverage — the 16/64 EWMAC rule, sizing at a 12% risk target in whole shares, a trailing stop, paper tickets with a compulsory journal, the account marked to market nightly with a risk report, the pre-trade checks (size on the current value at 12% ÷ N instruments, concentration, the budget), a quarantine for unexplained small-cap jumps. Each week shipped after an independent review (v0.9.0: 26 findings; v0.10.0: 29; v0.11.0: 25; all resolved on the record, the resolution tables in `docs/reviews/`), and each was closed on live evidence (VC-19, VC-20, VC-21 in `docs/data-provider-checks.md`). Three paper accounts are live.

## Method
- Read `README.md`, then `docs/syllabus.md` (the phase gates and decisions 17–20), then `docs/OPEN-ITEMS.md`, then the code, then the content; write probe tests for any suspicion before recording it (keep them under `.review-probes/`, never in `src/`; if you copy one into `src/` to run it, delete it after — `git status --short` must show only the two laid-over files, your report and `.review-probes/` when you finish).
- For a synthetic seed: ~320 trading days of geometric-random closes per core symbol into `daily_prices (symbol, date, close, adj_open, adj_high, adj_low, adj_close, volume)` with `tickers (symbol, name, sector, status[, tier, cik])` rows; a small-tier symbol gets `tier = 'small'`; an `av_jump` receipt is a `fetch_log` row with `outcome = 'av_jump'` and the text `jumpReceipt()` in `src/lib/adjust.ts` writes. Migration 0009 seeds one `splits` row (MLI, 2026-07-01, factor 2).
- The outbound network is restricted (package registries only): no provider calls; everything local; the providers are stubbed at `fetch` in the tests.
- Severity: **must fix** (wrong result, unsafe, or something a successor could not recover from), **should fix** (misleading, fragile, or housekeeping that will cost more later), **note** (record it; no change needed). Every finding: evidence (file and line, or a probe you ran, or a page), the rule it breaks, a recommendation, and what it would cost to fix. Housekeeping items are findings too, with the same discipline.
- Output: write `docs/reviews/phase-3-independent-review.md` in the review tree — verdict; findings table (F for must/should, N for notes, H for housekeeping items); the three rules read together; arithmetic and tests; the Worker on the real local D1 (what you ran, what you saw); the record's consistency (what was checked file by file); the Phase 3 story; what was checked and found correct; Phase 4 readiness; method and limits. Change nothing else. Do not fix anything. Do not read any file outside the review tree except the npm packages it depends on; the builder's working copies and conversation are not yours to see.
