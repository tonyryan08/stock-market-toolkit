# Independent review — Phase 3 (weeks 7–9) and the repository's housekeeping

**Reviewed:** the public repository at commit `1950b03` with the two laid-over files (`docs/how-we-built-it-phase-3.md`, `README.md`) — a fresh clone, the builder's working copies and conversation out of bounds. **Date:** 10 Nov 2026. **Brief:** `_REVIEW-BRIEF-PHASE-3.md`. **Rule:** findings only; nothing in the tree was changed; the probes are under `.review-probes/` and never touched `src/`.

## Verdict

**Phase 3 is sound and the gate stands. Ship Phase 4 on it, with one must-fix in the account arithmetic first.** The three rules read together are one coherent system: one table for the rule, one for sizing, one `markToMarket` behind the ticket, the strip, the report and the nightly row; the night's date is taken from one place; the receipts one job writes are parsed by the function that writes them. The 445 tests pass on any date, in any time zone and locale; `tsc` is clean; the ten migrations apply to a fresh D1 in sequence; the Worker ran three synthetic nights on a real local D1 with three enrolled students and every refusal, stamp, re-alignment, quarantine and report row came out as the record says, to the cent. The one must (F1) is a seam between week 8 and week 9 that no single week's review could see: after a dividend re-alignment the account value is understated by the dividend while the position is held — the page's own figures stop adding up by exactly units × the dividend, and the first live ex-date on a held name will show it. It is a one-line fix with a one-line test. Six shoulds follow; the housekeeping list is long but mechanical.

Counts: **1 must · 6 should · 16 notes · 14 housekeeping items.**

## The three rules read together

I read `signals.ts` → `jobs/signals.ts` → `sizing.ts` → `tickets.ts` → `portfolio.ts` → `jobs/stops.ts` → `lib/risk.ts` → `jobs/risk.ts` → `jobs/nightly.ts` as one system, looking for the seams the brief names.

- **A value computed two ways.** `markToMarket` (`src/lib/tickets.ts` 145–178) is the one arithmetic behind `currentValue` (the ticket), the strip, `buildReport` (the page and the API) and the nightly row — the v0.11.0 F4 fix holds; I verified on the local D1 that `/api/risk`, the page and the `risk_reports` row agree. The seam that remains is F1: `accountOf` (week 8's ledger) and `markToMarket` (week 9's mark) agree only until the tracker re-aligns an entry. "Since enrolment" is the one figure computed twice (N7), identically.
- **A check one week assumes and another removed.** None. The week-8 refusals (rule, held, re-entry, risk, size, oversized, cash, journal, partial, none) all survive in week 9's `decideTicket`, in the same order, with the three week-9 checks (tier gate, concentration, budget) inserted where decision #20 says.
- **Order of operations.** Tier gate (quarantine, then warming) returns before everything for a small cap (`tickets.ts` 196–199); then rule → held → re-entry → risk → concentration → size/oversized → budget (skipped when held or concentration already refuses — F5 of v0.11.0) → cash. Verified live: a held name asked for twice the size reads `held` + `oversized`; a flat name with a position open elsewhere reads `rule` + `concentration`; a small cap mature and long with N full reads `concentration` alone.
- **A receipt one job writes and another reads.** `av_jump`: `jumpReceipt`/`parseJumpReceipt` sit together; the visit test writes the receipt the quarantine reads. `run_summary.avCalls` and `phase`: written by the visit, read by `rationLeft` with `json_valid` guards. `quote_demo`: written by the quote route, counted by both the cap and the ration.
