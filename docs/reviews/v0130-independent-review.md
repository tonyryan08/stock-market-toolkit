# Independent review — v0.13.0 (week 11: the process review — explain-my-portfolio; the costs-and-tax worksheet)

**Reviewed:** the public repository at commit `2240884` (v0.12.0) with the week-11 shipment laid over it — 35 paths plus the shipment notes, a fresh clone, the builder's working copies and conversation out of bounds. **Date:** 12 Nov 2026. **Brief:** `_REVIEW-BRIEF-WEEK-11.md`. **Rule:** findings only; nothing in the tree was changed except this file (the tree was confirmed identical to the start: `git status --short` 36 lines plus the two `_` files and the `node_modules` link; the three mutated files byte-identical to their originals; 553 tests, `tsc` clean, at the end as at the start).

## Verdict

**Ship with changes.** The week is built the way the record says it is in the parts that matter most: the sheet the model reads carries none of the toolkit's P&L, account-value or drawdown figures (checked on the fixture, the seeded book and the Worker's own request body); the audit is a pure function over the rows and the page always shows it; a page view makes no model call; "nothing yet" makes no call; the caps are counted insert-first and held under a ten-request burst (3 answered, 7 capped, 3 model calls, no pending row left); the receipts carry no code; praise is withheld; a 500 is an error with the audit intact and a key-shaped string in the upstream text is redacted; the worksheet's arithmetic agrees with `TAX_RULES`, the lesson, the kit, the deck and the budget notes to the cent; the decks carry the line on every slide; the pins are unchanged and `npm audit` is clean.

Three things contradict the record in ways a student or a successor would act on, and each is a small fix: (1) the record says a reading with a number from nowhere, an unknown fact id or an uncited paragraph is **withheld** — the code shows it, flagged, and stores it as the last reading; (2) the shipped notes seed was generated before the last two edits to the files it is cut from — it carries lesson 10's old text, lacks the quickstart's week-11 section, and the corpus is 193 passages, not 192; (3) the runbook says that on enactment setting `checkedOn` and the `basis` fields makes the worksheet page follow — the page's six "Bill" marks and its sentences are literals and do not.

Counts: **3 must · 7 should · 17 notes · 13 housekeeping items.** 47 mutations: 36 killed, 11 survived (9 of them gaps to pin — F7, F8, F4, N1, N10, H6's no-key guard; 2 harmless — H6's arithmetic edges).
