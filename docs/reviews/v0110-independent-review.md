## Verdict

**Ship with changes.** The mark-to-market is right to the cent (the ledger's cash, Σ units × latest close, open and realised P&L, the mark carried and only rising, the drawdown from it, risk used as Σ weight × instrument risk, the nearest stop, the five flags), the three week-9 checks on the ticket do what decision #20 says for N = 1…4 (the rule's size on the current value at 12% ÷ N, the (N+1)th name refused by concentration, the budget biting only when a share got wilder or N fell), the nightly pass writes a row per student in its own fence with the receipts the notes promise, the quarantine reads the receipts and the splits table and the `Q` appears, the page carries no code, every interpolated value is escaped, the migration is safe, the enrol script is strict, the hygiene is clean and the record's arithmetic closes (one five-cent line aside). On the real local D1 the Phase 3 gate's three sentences were shown: two students opened rule-compliant positions, the oversized and the concentrated tickets were refused with their sentences, and the `risk` pass wrote and the page rendered a report per student that a calculator reproduces.

**Must fix before the apply (1):** the quarantine's parser reads only the **first** jump of an `av_jump` receipt. The visit prints `×` once, before the first ratio (`adjusted close moved ×2.01 on 2026-06-10, 0.48 on 2026-08-20 …`), and `quarantineReason`'s regex requires the `×`; a receipt naming two jumps with the first explained by a splits row leaves the symbol unquarantined — on the local D1 such a symbol showed no `Q`, the calculator sized it and an opening ticket was **accepted** (63 shares), which is the one outcome OI-08's design exists to prevent. One regex character and two tests.

**Should fix (10):** the nightly row is keyed on the close the account is *marked to*, not the night — a student whose only position's symbol lags (a small cap between visits, a core symbol FMP skipped, a US holiday for everyone) gets no row for the night and the previous close's row is overwritten with tonight's state (decision #20 g says "one row per student per night"); the quarantine scans the whole `fetch_log` once per small-tier symbol per night (`SCAN fetch_log`, ~48 × the table's rows read nightly, growing); the report and the ticket count a position with no instrument risk differently (the page says 0.0% used, the budget check sees 11.6%); the "budget" reason is appended to `held`/`concentration` refusals with a remedy that cannot cure them; a freshly enrolled page reads "as of the — close" until the first nightly row (every VC-21 student between step 5 and 23:00 UTC); the calculator sizes at the whole 12% whatever N the student runs, against the card's formula; three VC-21 lines will not read as written ("to the cent" from a two-decimal close, the instrument risk "on /signals" rounded to a whole percent, "the gate's three sentences are a–c"); the drawdown statement is labelled "deliberately conservative" when it is the typical case, slide 8's figures are the sandbox's synthetic Apple that no test pins, and the lesson's budget example needs a Coca-Cola volatility above 100%; and the week-9 BUILD-LOG entry's per-file test counts do not add up. **Notes (14).**
