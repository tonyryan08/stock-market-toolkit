## Verdict

**Ship with changes.** The sizing arithmetic is Carver's Starter System (12% target, exposure = capital × target ÷ instrument risk, capped at the account, whole shares, the stop at half the annual risk below the high since entry with the gap fixed at entry, one closing rule), the departures are stated in decision #19, the worked examples are right to the cent, the ticket and the stop tracker do what the lesson says on the real local D1 (the batch's `last_insert_rowid()` links the position to its ticket; the trailing, the stamp, the re-alignment all land), the migration is safe over a live database, the record is honest and its counts are right, and the hygiene is clean. It can be taught — after four small fixes, because each of them sits exactly on the path the exit check walks.

**Must fix before the apply (4):** `minimumCapital` names a figure that the calculator then refuses — the lesson's "$1,212" for Apple fails the calculator's own cost check (the true minimum under the shipped rules is $1,586; KO $1,490, MSFT $1,993), so the page contradicts itself and the card, the lesson and the deck carry the wrong number; the "Oversized" sentence — the exit check itself — is never shown to a student who already holds the position, which is the state every student is in after the lesson's step 1 and the state VC-20 (c) is written for; that sentence prints the instrument risk to one decimal (18.2%) while the arithmetic used 18.18%, so "check it on a calculator" is off by $7; and the ration planner's `json_extract` throws `malformed JSON` on any `run_summary` receipt that v0.9.0 cut at 300 characters, unfenced, so the first visit after the apply can end with no receipt and a 14-minute wait.

**Should fix, next shipment (10):** the tracker re-aligns every open position every night after its first genuine re-alignment (cents v. six decimals); a split after entry rescales the price but not the share count; the ration window is measured from a run's start, not its calls; one position per instrument is a read-then-write (a double-click opens two); `esc` leaves `"` alone, so both forms can be made to grow attributes; the leverage slide's arithmetic ("the same 18 shares with $5,000") is not Carver's; the enrol script refuses Seán and Ó Briain; a reverse split is silently ignored; the calculator sizes small-cap and warming instruments without a word; three VC-20 lines (e, f, g) will not read as written. **Notes (15).**
