## Verdict

**Ship with changes — F1–F3 and S1–S2 before the zip.** The door is sound: every planner route answers 403 to a signed-in student and to an Access identity with no email, before anything is read or written, and the tutor and the door-off state get the page. The ranking, the sizes at the enrolled N on tonight's value, the stops, the euro arithmetic, the CSV quoting and formula guard, and the escaping all check. But decision #24 (a)'s central promise — *"the planner cannot propose what the ticket would refuse without saying so"* — does not hold. The cash fit and the sizes are taken on the **typed** capital, and the page writes the old figure back into that field after every raise and into every saved plan, so the plan routinely shows a clean row that the judge refuses (F1). The rows are also sized on one capital while each accepted ticket lowers the account by its cost (F3). And the prefilled journal, which ends at "Thesis:", is accepted by the judge as the ticket: the tool writes the "why" that the course says is the student's (F2). **Counts: 3 must · 4 should · 9 notes · 6 housekeeping.**

## Must

### F1 — The cash fit and the sizes use the typed capital, not the account the judge reads, and the page refills that field with a stale figure

- **Where:** `src/lib/plan.ts:211` (`let cash = Math.max(0, inputs.capital - (open.invested ?? 0))`), `:227` (`sizePosition({ capital: inputs.capital, … })`); `src/plan.ts:110` (`invested = value − cash`), `:356` (the capital field's `value` is `p.inputs.capital`), `:102` (a saved plan's inputs replace tonight's).
- **What I saw:** the planner's cash is `capital − (value − cash)`, which equals the ledger cash only when the typed capital equals tonight's value. Three ways it does not, two of them without anyone typing anything:
  1. **After a raise.** The page rebuilt after *Raise the tickets* keeps the capital as posted (the pre-raise $10,000), while the account is now worth $9,986.03 with $5,199.56 cash. The page's own notes then read *"Not enough cash after the rows above: $5,213.53 left"*. That is $13.97 more than the account holds, which is exactly the first ticket's cost.
  2. **A saved plan.** *Save* stores the capital as a concrete number (the form always posts the field filled), and every later GET sizes and fits on it. I saved DEAR × 52 at capital 10000 after GOOD was open. The next GET showed *"a saved plan is shown"*, capital 10000, **no problem on the row**. *Raise* → **0 of 1 accepted**: *"Not enough cash: $5,199.56 available ($4,786.47 in 1 open position(s) at the latest close); 52 shares of DEAR at $99.83 plus the $14.98 cost is $5,206.18. 51 shares would fit, cost included."*
  3. **A what-if capital.** Capital 20000 with NEAR blank → the row reads *"Fitted to the cash left after the rows above: the rule's size is 267 shares, 202 fit (cost included)"*, with no warning. The account has $5,199.56 and the judge's rule size is 133. *Raise* → *"Oversized. … an account worth $9,986.03 supports $9,986.03 of NEAR — 133 shares at $74.83. You asked for 202 …"*.
  
  The same applies to a typed N other than the enrolled one: the plan sizes at 12% ÷ typed N, and the judge sizes at the enrolled N. The input's small print says the judge uses the enrolled N, but no row flags it.
- **Why it matters:** this is the arithmetic disagreement decision #24 (a) says cannot happen. The judge still refuses, so no wrong position is written. But the planner's numbers are wrong as shown: "202 fit" with $5,199 in cash. The page and every saved plan produce them, not only a deliberate what-if.
- **What I'd change:** always compute the judge's facts beside the typed ones. That means the cash fit from `l.open.cash` (the ledger), the rule's units at `l.open.value` and the enrolled N, and per row "the ticket sizes on $X at N = n: it would refuse this / allow up to k". Treat a typed capital or N as a labelled what-if column, not as the basis of the fit. Save `capital: null` (meaning "tonight's value") unless the user typed something different from the default, and do not echo the pre-raise capital back into the rebuilt form. Pin with a test: a saved plan from yesterday's value plus an open position must flag the cash exactly as `decideTicket` does.

### F2 — The prefilled journal is raised as the ticket; the machine's "Thesis: " passes the judge

- **Where:** `src/lib/plan.ts:176–179` (`prefillJournal` ends `… Thesis: `), `:258` (`journal: p.journal.trim() || prefillJournal(…)`); `src/plan.ts:171` (`handleTicket(…, journal: r.journal)`); the judge's only test is length ≥ 20 (`src/lib/tickets.ts:95`).
- **What I saw:** *Raise* with GOOD × 80 and its journal left as the page offered it. The ticket was **accepted**, and the `tickets` row's journal is exactly the prefill: *"GOOD: rule LONG since 2026-10-09 (forecast +20.0); Graham 7/7 · 4/6; 80 of the rule's 167 shares at the 6% target; stop $59.09 (gap $0.74), trailing the high; if it is hit I close on the next close. Thesis:"*. Nothing follows "Thesis:". The textarea is also pre-populated (`src/plan.ts:325`), so a browser user who types nothing sends the prefill (two textareas on the first GET end `Thesis: </textarea>`).
- **Why it matters:** "the journal entry is the ticket" is the course's rule (`tickets.ts` header, lesson 8), and decision #24 (e) says the planner prefills the facts and *the writer adds the thesis*. As built, the writer need not. Week 11's process audit then credits the entry: it names the rule and the stop by construction, so `auditProcess`'s word tests pass a journal no one wrote. The account's record shows a decision that was not made.
- **What I'd change:** refuse a planner row whose journal has nothing after the prefill's "Thesis:" (or whose length beyond the prefill is under `JOURNAL_MIN`) before calling `handleTicket`, with a sentence under the row ("write the thesis — what would prove you wrong"). Better still, render the facts as a read-only line above an empty textarea and join them at raise time. Pin: a raise with the untouched prefill → refused, no `INSERT INTO tickets`.

### F3 — The rows are sized on one capital; each accepted ticket lowers the value by its cost, and the judge sizes the next row on the lower figure

- **Where:** `src/lib/plan.ts:227` (every row on `inputs.capital`) against `src/lib/tickets.ts:225, 237` (`currentValue` after the previous ticket: cash fell by exposure + cost, market value rose by exposure only).
- **What I saw (pure functions, default inputs, a fresh $10,000 account, N = 2):** AAA $50 at 20% and BBBB $100 at 30%. `buildPlan` → `[AAA 60, BBBB 20]`, both with **no problems**. `decideTicket` in order: AAA *accepted 60*; BBBB *"Oversized. At 30.00% instrument risk and the 6% target (12% ÷ 2 instruments), an account worth $9,990.50 supports $1,998.10 of BBBB — 19 shares at $100.00. You asked for 20 …"*.
- **Why it matters:** this is a refusal the plan did not predict, on the planner's main path (blank units, Raise). It is infrequent: it bites when a row's target sits within roughly (the rows above's costs × target ÷ risk) of a whole-share boundary — about 1–2% of second rows for a $100 share at 30% risk, less for dearer shares. But it is the exact disagreement #24 (a) rules out, and the page gives no warning.
- **What I'd change:** size row k on the capital less the opening costs of rows 1…k−1, which is what the judge will see if they are raised in rank order. Raise them in that order (it already does). Pin the AAA/BBBB pair above.

## Should

### S1 — *Save* never keeps a blank units cell or a blank journal from the browser; the "re-sizes each night" claims do not hold

- **Where:** `src/plan.ts:304, 318` (the units input's `value` is the computed units, fitted or not), `:325` (the textarea holds the prefill), `:160–162` (the comment: "a blank units cell stays blank, so the saved plan re-sizes … a blank journal stays blank, so the prefill carries tomorrow's facts"); README planner paragraph (*"the plan re-sizes with the rule each night"*); OI-102 and setup guide F1 (*"the picks as typed"*).
- **What I saw:** the first GET's form carries `units_GOOD` = 166 and `units_NEAR` = 0 as values, and both journals filled with the prefill. Whatever the browser posts on *Save* is therefore concrete units and the prefill text (the saved row I wrote held `"units":52` and the typed journal). A blank cell survives only from a hand-built POST. One *Recompute* has the same effect: the fitted 202 of F1 (3) came back as a typed 202. A saved plan then carries yesterday's units, the stop and gap in yesterday's prefill (wrong once the price moves), and, with F1, yesterday's capital.
- **What I'd change:** show the rule's or fitted size as the input's `placeholder` and keep `value` for what the user typed; do the same for the journal (F2's fix covers it). Then the code, the comment and the README agree. Pin: GET → the form's units inputs are empty for rows the user has not typed.

### S2 — An unreadable units entry silently becomes the rule's full (fitted) size

- **Where:** `src/plan.ts:88` (`/^\d{1,7}$/` or `null`, and `null` means "the rule's").
- **What I saw:** `units_NEAR=5O` (a letter O) → the row shows **69** (fitted) and no problem. The same happens for `-3` on GOOD (→ 166). *Raise* would send that figure.
- **Why it matters:** the user asked for few and gets the most the rules allow. "Every cell the user's to overwrite" should not mean that a typo is overwritten with the maximum.
- **What I'd change:** treat a non-blank units cell that is not a whole number ≥ 1 as an input error for that row (422, or a row problem that blocks its raise), in `parseTicket`'s words: *"Units must be a whole number of shares, at least 1."*

### S3 — VC-25 (g) and setup F4 describe a second raise that the page does not produce

- **Where:** `docs/data-provider-checks.md:440` (VC-25 g: *"a second raise of the same name → 'already held' under the row"*); `src/plan.ts:83–93` (picks come only from eligible candidates), `:165`.
- **What I saw:** with GOOD open, *Raise* with `pick_GOOD=on` → flash **"Nothing to raise: no row is ticked."**, and the notes say *"GOOD dropped: already held: 80 shares opened 2026-11-10 — one position per instrument."* A held name is in the "Not eligible" table, which has no checkbox, so in a browser it cannot be ticked at all. No ticket is tried and nothing appears "under the row". (After the first raise, the rebuilt page does list GOOD under "Not eligible" with "already held". That is what the BUILD-LOG observed, but it is not a second raise.)
- **What I'd change:** reword VC-25 (g) and F4 to what the page does: after the raise the name moves to "Not eligible" with "already held", and it cannot be ticked again. Make the flash count ticked-but-dropped rows: "Nothing raised: 1 ticked row is not eligible tonight (see the notes)."

### S4 — An input error discards every tick, units cell and journal the user typed

- **Where:** `src/plan.ts:152` (`planPage(l, currentPlan(l), …)` on `PlanInputError`).
- **What I saw:** `capital=-5` (or `eurusd=0`, `positions=2.5`, `spread=-1`) → **422** with the right sentence (e.g. *"capital must be a number from 100 to 1000000000."*). The table below it is the saved or top plan, not the user's: their picks, units and journals (up to 1,000 characters each) are gone.
- **What I'd change:** on a bad input, rebuild the plan from the form's picks with the defaults for the bad field, and keep the form's raw values in the inputs, with the error flash above.

## Notes

- **N1 — "Never a return" and the forecast.** The third sort key is the EWMAC forecast, which is a scaled difference of two moving averages of past prices over their volatility. That is a risk-adjusted measure of the recent price trend. The design names it (#24 b), and the claim is literally true: no return figure is used. But within a band, after the Graham counts, the order is the recent trend. On the seed every forecast is capped at +20, so it never decided an order here. Suggest the lede say what the forecast is ("the rule's trend strength, from the price series") rather than leave "never a return" to be read as "nothing price-based".
- **N2 — The raise loop's bound is 4, not the account's room.** `src/plan.ts:168` slices to `SIZING.maxInstruments`, while the comment says "at most N rows". With one position open and N = 2, ticking NEAR, DEAR and NOBK tried all three: one was accepted and two were refused *"Concentration: your account runs 2 instruments and 2 positions are open (GOOD, NEAR)…"*. The plan had said so first, as (e) promises, but the refusals were spent anyway. Either `slice(0, totals.roomFor)` (and say which rows were left) or fix the comment.
- **N3 — A zero-unit row.** When nothing fits, the row reads **"round trip Infinity%"** (`src/plan.ts:322`; `roundTripPct` is `Infinity` and `toFixed` prints it), and its prefill says *"0 of the rule's 133 shares"*. Raising it sends `"0"`, which the judge refuses as *"Units must be a whole number of shares, at least 1."* rather than in the cash words the row printed. Print a dash and do not raise a 0 row.
- **N4 — No cross-site check on `POST /plan/:code`.** It is the same pattern as the ticket form, which was pre-existing and noted as unchecked in the v1.0.0 review. But this POST is the tutor's and can write a ticket into any account. Whether a cross-site form post carries the Access session depends on the Access application's cookie SameSite setting, which I did not verify. An `Origin`/`Sec-Fetch-Site` check on the planner's POST is a few lines.
- **N5 — `parseSavedPlan` does not re-apply the input bounds.** It accepts capital 5 or 1e300, `eurUsd` 1e-300, `spreadPct` 1e308, `units` 1e21, journals of any length and any number of picks. On those shapes `buildPlan` produced a 1e21-share row, a euro value of `Infinity` (JSON `null`, "€∞" on the page) and a cash-after of −6e22. Nothing threw, and the CSV was written. It is reachable only through a hand-edited or future-format row, but the parser is the last guard. Run the stored inputs through `parseInputs`' bounds and cap the units at 1,000,000 like `parseTicket`.
- **N6 — The number parser is lenient.** `[€$,\s%]` are stripped anywhere, so `1,0,0` → 100. `Number()` reads hex (`0x2710` → 10000) and exponents (`1e9`). A euro-formatted `10.000,50` → 10.0005 is refused, which is the right outcome by luck. A plain-decimal regex, as `parseTicket` uses for units, would be predictable.
- **N7 — The euro labels.** The totals' "€ … to open" counts commission and conversion but not the spread, while "round trips" includes the spread once. "At risk to the stops" excludes the exit's costs. The arithmetic is right (checked by hand: GOOD × 80 at 1.08 → €4,431.92, stop €54.71, €54.81 at risk, trip €30.59). The labels would read better as "commission + conversion to open" and "to the stop, before costs".
- **N8 — "The ticket's reason" is the planner's paraphrase.** Decision #24 (b) says a refused name is listed with the ticket's reason. `ineligibleReason` writes its own sentences (e.g. *"quarantined: <reason>"* against the judge's *"QUAR is quarantined: … No opening ticket until a splits row explains the jump …"*). The substance agrees in every case I traced (quarantine is core-exempt in both because only the small tier is ever quarantined; warming, flat, held, fresh-crossover and no-risk all match). Either reword (b) or reuse the judge's sentences.
- **N9 — A failed save is a 200, and a failed read is silent.** *Save* with the table dropped answers 200 with the right flash. `loadSaved` turns any database error into "nothing saved yet", so on a blip the tutor sees the top names and may think the saved plan is gone. Also, the stop warning (#24 c, "printed beside it") is in the rule box at the foot, not beside the stop column.

## Checked and found sound

- **The door.** `plannerCheck` opens for the door off or the tutor only. A student, an emailless identity, and the tutor's absence from `TUTOR_EMAILS` all get 403. `guard` checks the door before `loadStudent`, so a student cannot tell a real code from a missing one (403 for both). The CSV and the API share the guard. `POST` raise goes through the same guard before the body is read for tickets. `handleTicket` is called with the URL's code only (the form cannot name another account). `/me`'s landing link shows for the tutor only.
- **The order.** `rankCandidates` matches #24 (b) and the printed lede: band, then defensive passes, enterprising passes, forecast, lower risk, symbol. No price change, P&L or return field is read.
- **Eligibility mirrors the judge.** Quarantine, warming, flat, no risk, held and fresh crossover use the same conditions as `decideTicket` (the crossover test `state_since < closed_on` is identical). The gate rows and the judge read the same latest row per symbol (`MAX(date)` per symbol against `ORDER BY date DESC LIMIT 1`), active tickers only.
- **The sizes at the defaults.** `sizePosition` at `targetFor(enrolled N)` on `max(100, value)`. The rule's units, the oversized test (`units > s.units`), the one-share and 1% cost problems (with the judge's "not checked" suppression), and the cash "would fit" loop are the judge's. The stop gap and level are `sizing.ts`'s rounded figures, the same the ticket stores (verified on the accepted ticket: 0.74 / 59.09).
- **The budget and count notes** use the judge's thresholds (12 + 1 point, `1e-9`) and `markToMarket`'s risk used.
- **The euro lines** (#24 f): €2 an order, 0.25% each way on the euro value, the spread once at the typed rate, the connectivity fee named once and not apportioned. Blank without a rate, with the note. No FX fetched anywhere.
- **The CSV.** RFC 4180 quoting (quote, comma, CR/LF), CRLF line ends, the apostrophe guard on `= + - @ \t \r` for every string cell (the name, problems and notes as well as the journal), numbers unquoted, `Infinity` written as blank, `content-disposition` attachment, `no-store`.
- **Escaping.** Every string interpolated into the planner's HTML goes through `esc` (which escapes `"`): symbols, names, the reasons, the notes, the flash, the journal, the as-of date, the email, the code in hrefs and form actions. The unescaped interpolations are numbers or the fixed band words. The form's field names (`pick_/units_/journal_<SYMBOL>`, `capital`, `positions`, `eurusd`, `spread`, `action`) match the parser.
- **The raise loop.** It is bounded twice: by the eligible candidates (the form's ticks are matched only against tonight's rows, so 90 ticks become at most 7 rows) and by 4. It is sequential, with the judge loading fresh facts each time. Accepted names drop out of the rebuilt page.
- **The migration.** One row per code (primary key), `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`, the computed figures never stored. A missing table is said on *Save* and tolerated on read.
- **The record:** 623 / 33 / 26 tests, migrations 0014, the version 1.1.0 in `package.json` and both lockfile fields, decision #24's text, OI-103–106, and VC-25 (a)–(f) and (h) match the code. The exceptions are S1, S3, H1, H2 and H4.
