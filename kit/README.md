# The takeaway kit

Intro to the Stock Market · the documents a student leaves with. Every one is read by the course's tutor (`/tutor`), and `/kit` on the site lists them with these links.

| | Document | From | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Student quickstart](student-quickstart.md) | week 1, kept current | What you need, how the site grows week by week, how to sign in and find your page, and why the code is public. |
| 2 | [The decision journal](decision-journal.md) | week 1 | The template every ticket's journal entry follows: the trigger, the thesis, what would prove you wrong, the size and why, when you will judge it. |
| 3 | [The Starter System — the rules card](starter-system-rules-card.md) | weeks 7–9 | The seven rules the toolkit enforces, one page: the opening rule, the risk target, sizing, the stop, costs, the Monday routine, what the ticket refuses. |
| 4 | [The reading list](reading-list.md) | week 1, grown since | Graham, Carver, Peacock and the rest — what each is for and which chapters the course leans on. |
| 5 | [Costs and tax — the walkthrough](costs-and-tax-walkthrough.md) | week 11 | A broker's mechanics and the Irish costs and tax on a paper trade, every figure dated, the Bill marked as a Bill. |
| 6 | [Scam-spotting notes](scam-spotting-notes.md) | weeks 6 and 11 | Stories versus numbers, and the platform itself: the register, the number, the fee page, the exit — the red flags one breath each. |
| 7 | [Demo day — the rubric and the retrospective](demo-day.md) | week 12 | How one decision is presented and judged on process alone, and the questions the course asks of itself at the end. |

**To print for the room:** the rules card and the journal template (one each), the costs-and-tax walkthrough and the scam-spotting notes (one per pair), demo day's rubric (one each, week 12).

*Education, not advice. Nothing in this kit is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.*
